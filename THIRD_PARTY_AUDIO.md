## Salamander Grand Piano V3

Realistic piano samples used by the realistic-piano build are derived from
Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm.

Instrument: Yamaha C5 grand piano
Original author: Alexander Holm
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
Original project: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
Original archive: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3

The build workflow downloads the compact Salamander sample distribution used by
Tone.js and converts those recordings to Ogg Vorbis for Minecraft packaging.

No ownership is claimed over the Salamander recordings.
